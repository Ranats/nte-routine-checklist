# Privacy

This checklist is local-first.

- No login.
- No game account ID collection.
- No email address collection.
- No screenshot upload.
- No analytics SDK.
- Checklist state, settings, and hidden/custom items are stored in browser `localStorage`.
- Export and import only handle local JSON files selected by the user.
- The production site may load Google AdSense for site review or ads.
- Display ads are limited to the reserved sponsored area when a slot ID is configured.
- The app does not send checklist data to an app backend.

Before adding analytics, additional ads, or other third-party scripts, update this policy with:

- the provider name,
- the data sent,
- the purpose,
- consent and opt-out behavior,
- regional cookie/tracking requirements.
