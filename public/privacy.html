Privacy

This checklist is local-first. It does not include login, analytics, account linking, or an app backend.

Checklist state, settings, hidden items, and custom items are stored in browser localStorage.

Export and import only handle local JSON files selected by the user.

The production site may load Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve or measure ads.

The app does not send checklist data to a backend.

Before analytics, additional ads, or other third-party scripts are added, this page must be updated with provider, data, consent, and opt-out details.
